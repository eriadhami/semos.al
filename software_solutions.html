m-Bank
m-Bank
Our mobile banking application, enables the clients to manage and review the status of their accounts and perform transactions with funds available on their mobile devices as well as provide a set of useful additional tools and informations.
Through this application user can access its personal bank profile, review the balances of personal bank accounts and process transactions according different payment orders. Each payment order must be signed with token (hardware or software token, which is creating onetime OTP password). The application uses a set of informative data available to all users on their mobile application. The user has access to information such as closest bank address, draw route to the closest bank, closest ATM locations. Also there are additional M-Banking functionalities available like currency converter, exchange rates (for certain date), credit calculators, deposit calculators, etc.
The mobile banking application, offers access to different subscribed services, like mobile, SMS or e-mail notifications. These notifications are directly related to all transactions that a user can process.

Purchase Management System
Purchase Management System is software solution which provides management of the purchase requests from all organizational units in entire enterprise.
The organizational units can purchase items or services.
The enterprise defines purchase types and the approval workflow for each purchase type.
In the approval processes there are specific information for each purchase requests like company budget information, account real and implicated values, suppliers' bids, choosing the most favorable bid, group approval by category or chosen supplier etc.

IFRS
IFRS
An International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS) framework is the foundation of accounting standards.

The framework states that the objective of financial statements is to provide information about the financial position, performance and changes in the financial position of an entity that is useful to a wide range of users in making economic decisions, and to provide the current financial status of the entity to its shareholders and public in general.
